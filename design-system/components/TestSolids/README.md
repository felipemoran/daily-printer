# TestSolids

Paper test for large solid areas and heavy display type (item 11 of the To verify list).

- Solid bands of 8, 16, 40 and 120px; the header bar; a reversed stat tile; the Severity block with its 172px numeral; hazard tape; a 900-weight `display-xl` line.
- Look for: smear below solids and heavy strokes, striping inside bands at full speed, evenness of large blocks, white text closing up inside ink.
- Result decides how much the templates lean on solids.
