# Masthead

The receipt title: a condensed display word or ID, an optional right-aligned date block, an optional subline, and the 8px `stroke-bar` rule.

- Provide: `title`, `size` (`"xl"` 92px for IDs and day names, `"lg"` 79px default, `"md"` 63px for two-line titles), `aside` (array of short lines, right aligned, bold), `sub` (one bold line), `rule={false}` to omit the rule.
- One masthead per receipt. Keep titles to one or two words — condensed 900 fits about 9 characters at `lg`.
